## Eligibility decision and unresolved points

The enacted section 2(a) uses 5 November 2025 through 31 December 2026.
The current official service independently confirms acceptance of applications
through **10 February 2027**. This administrative fact is not ignored or described
as an amendment to the printed statute. A newer circular or published clarification
settling the detailed treatment of 2027 arrivals was not located during the audit.

Accordingly, January 1-February 10, 2027 returns a distinct extension **review**
state, with no calculated personal ceiling or entitlement and no invented 2027
prorating rule. Dates outside either window also require review, explaining the
restriction without claiming a definitive zero legal exemption.

Toshav Hozer Vatik always requires document and ten-year foreign-residence review.
Company/62a income, passive income, uncertain source, pre-residence income and
residence cessation are outside the automatic personal-income calculation.

For clear direct family payments, the calculation remains conditional on the
statutory classification. Company links and uncertain relationships require review.
The wholly-owned-company exception is explained, not automatically adjudicated.

For mixed related/non-related income the engine calculates only the combined
mathematical upper bound `min(generalCap, ordinary + min(related, relatedCap))`.
It never assigns an exemption to a particular payer. If that sum exceeds the global
cap, the result is review, with the bound explicitly shown only as a conditional
illustration. This is conservative scope, not a claim that the law denies relief.

## Calculation and presentation

- Fiscal caps: 600,000 / 1,000,000 / 1,000,000 / 350,000 / 150,000 ILS.
- 2026 only: actual eligible residence days, inclusive, capped at the calendar year,
  divided by 365. No 2025 benefit or factor above one.
- Related cap: 140,000 in 2026-2029, same 2026 residence prorata; 150,000 in 2030.
- Related income is a **subset** of total annual income in the UI, not an extra
  amount added to it. Disabled related inputs contribute zero, even after editing.
- No unused-cap carryforward, intermediate rounding, savings estimate or final tax.
- Review/invalid results return `null`, not zero, for exempt and remaining income.
- No legal certification checkbox. No client-side storage, server submission or
  added external script. WhatsApp text is generic and contains no entered amounts
  or residence dates; the CTA was not sent during QA.
- Reset, example, live calculation, source explanations and native print action
  use existing React/Tailwind calculator conventions. No global CSS was added.
