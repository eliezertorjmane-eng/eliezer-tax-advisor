## 52% adjustment and review gate

For qualifying independent activity, solve `adjustedBase + 0.52 * NI(insuredBase) = monthlyProfit`,
where insuredBase includes the combined minimum, salary priority and remaining cap. Health is never deducted.
An 80-iteration monotone solver avoids treating capped high-income contributions as uncapped.
The standard uncapped result matches the Tax Authority/Bitouah Leumi published algebraic equation.

For a standard salaried employee with non-work-classified activity, the employee/non-work FAQ is the basis of
the estimate: subtract 25% of the average wage from the activity income even when salary is present, then apply
the available brackets and total insured-income ceiling after salary. The independent 52% formula is not copied
to that employee branch. No non-employed minimum is added to an employee already insured through salary.

Without salary, taxable non-work-classified activity is deliberately not assigned a final contribution. The public rates example
subtracts the exemption without a 52% adjustment, whereas section 345 and the separate independent-plus-passive
example describe deduction provisions. Those sources do not establish a consistent complete order for this
particular no-salary category and its minimums. A rates-only diagnostic keeps the exemption,
priority, cap and split visible under a clearly labeled review disclosure. Its deduction is labeled "A valider".
Its totals are not presented as monthly/annual/quarterly final estimates or included as an estimate in WhatsApp.
Professional validation of section 345 and minimum treatment is required to enable these no-salary final estimates.
A zero taxable base or fully consumed ceiling remains supported.

Classification uses exact 50% and 15% thresholds, 6,884.50 and 2,065.35 ILS, as in Claude's implementation.
The official pages display these as approximately 6,885 and 2,065 ILS. The existing project's rounded thresholds
are corrected so a fractional monthly income is classified against the actual percentages.

### Employee exemption regression follow-up

The source reference HTML still has `franchise = S > 0 ? 0 : C.FRANCHISE` (line 291). That gives 201.50 ILS
before whole-ILS rounding, displayed as 202, for salary 6,000 / annual activity profit 20,000 / 2 weekly hours.
The React engine already returned zero for that exact case before this follow-up. Its remaining bugs were the
blanket review gate hiding taxable employee results and use of the rounded 3,442 exemption at fractional boundaries.
The supplied reference is preserved as audit evidence; it is not the public calculator implementation.

Additional regressions cover the exact exemption boundary, split remaining brackets, low-salary minimum exceptions,
the legacy export, missing salary and unsupported categories. NI and health components are rounded to cents;
all displayed rows and periodic totals reconcile to those displayed components.

Amounts retain fractional adjusted bases, whereas the official simulator appears to truncate certain bases before
displaying NI/health and rounds its final monthly total to whole ILS. Tests compare those official examples with
a documented small tolerance and separately verify the unrounded statutory equation.

## Exclusions and data flow

Resident base credits apply to a full-year eligible male/female resident using the Father/Mother selector.
No Oleh Hadash, Milouim, locality, disability, custody, single-parent or other special credits are added.
The legacy child `totalPoints` field remains child-only; new `totalStandardPoints` includes base once.
Salary, income-tax and general credit-point logic are unchanged.

Insurance excludes non-residents, minors, retirement, disability/exemptions, homemaker/student categories,
special arrangements, additional income sources, partial-year activity, pensions, Koupat Gemel and Esek Zair.
The category checkbox suspends final amounts for unsupported categories.
WhatsApp data is encoded in an existing contact link and sent only after the user's own action; no identifiers,
storage, tracking or automatic financial-data transmission are introduced.
