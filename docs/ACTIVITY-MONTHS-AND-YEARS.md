# Activity months and verified pension years

The Bitouah Leumi input is the number of declared occupational months, not invoice months. For a single stable qualifying self-employed status, assessed income is allocated over those months before applying monthly classification thresholds, reduced and regular rates, minimum and maximum bases, and salary priority. Contributions cover only that occupational period; obligations outside it are excluded. Salary is assumed constant and insured throughout the year. Status changes and partial-year non-definition activity require review, rather than a fabricated precise liability. Esek Zair still uses its existing 70% starting base without the classic section 345 reduction. The annual income-tax and pension brackets are not prorated by this input.
