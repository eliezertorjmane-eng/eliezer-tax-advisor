## Rules and sources

- [Enacted law, chapter II, sections 2-4](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_366872.pdf): annual eligible professional income, two statutory rates, salary pension coordination, and year-end exceptions. The indexed enacted text was accessible; direct PDF navigation was geo-blocked during review.
- [Ministry of Finance explanation](https://haotzarsheli.mof.gov.il/Subject/Pages/Mandatory-Pension-for-Self-Employed.aspx): 4.45% to half the average wage and 12.55% from half to the full average wage. Historical insurance figures on this page are not used.
- [Bituah Leumi 2026 circular](https://www.btl.gov.il/Insurance/HozrimBituah/Hozrim/_מקדמות_ודמי_ביטוח_לעצמאים_2026__.pdf): monthly average wage 13,769 ILS. Thus annual pension thresholds are 82,614 and 165,228 ILS, not the separate Bituah Leumi reduced collection threshold.
- [Tax Authority 2025 annual-return guide](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-guide-knowyourright/he/Guides_IncomeTax_da-2025.pdf): pension payments made by or for the individual on insured employment income offset the independent requirement. This source supports coordination, not 2026 amounts.
- [Kol Zchut practical explanation](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/ביטוח_פנסיוני_לעובד_עצמאי): first VAT registration through June versus July onwards; fewer income-earning months alone is not a new-registration exemption.

Eligibility is determined at December 31: at least 21 and under 60; at least six months since first VAT registration. DOB on/before 1962-01-01 also satisfies the transitional age-55 exemption on 2017-01-01. Turning 21 or 60 during the year does not introduce an invented monthly proration. June 30 registration passes; July 1 reaches its six-month anniversary only on the following January 1.

## Formula and precision

```
P = max(0, actual annual eligible business profit)
B1 = min(P, 82614)
B2 = min(max(P - 82614, 0), 82614)
G = B1 * 0.0445 + B2 * 0.1255
O = exempt ? 0 : G
salaryOffset = min(O, qualifying annual salary pension deposits)
independentRequirement = O - salaryOffset
remaining = max(0, independentRequirement - independent pension deposits)
excess = max(0, total qualifying deposits - O)
```

No salary is subtracted from profit. No contribution is counted twice. Internal values are not rounded. Detailed bracket contributions show five decimals; summary amounts show cents. Monthly planning is O/12, not a monthly collection instruction. Maximum annual G is 14,044.38 ILS.

## Scope requiring professional review

The standard salary offset accepts documented eligible pension contributions from the employee and employer. The reviewed primary sources establish coordination but do not expressly resolve every severance, separate insurance-premium or mixed-product component. The calculator does not automatically credit severance (פיצויים), infer pension deposits from gross salary, or certify an unsegregated statement. Mixed components require professional verification; their universal inclusion or exclusion is not presented as independently established law.

Eligible profit is before pension, Keren Hishtalmut and the other excluded deductions, loss offsets and exemptions specified in the legislation; a previously adjusted accounting profit may need reconciliation. Closure, reopening and residency changes are factual exceptional-year inputs that suspend numerical liability. Special partial-year treatment, pension insurance adequacy and tax optimization are outside this tool.
